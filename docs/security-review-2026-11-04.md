not another scanner disposition. The root, evmd and speedtest manifests are

does not require this package. No other dependency version or replacement changes.

30-second deadline. It checks valid keys and bounded rejection of oversized
moduli/primes using the upstream synthetic fixtures, without real credentials.

embedded resources, consensus/state, SDK/Geth forks and their provenance are
